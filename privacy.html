Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

Summary

Clip Relay is a personal tool used only by its operator with their own TikTok account. It collects no data from anyone else.

What it accesses

  • Basic TikTok profile info (user.info.basic): used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Video upload (video.upload): used only to send the operator's own videos to their TikTok drafts.

It does not read your followers, messages, comments, analytics, or other videos.

What it stores

The TikTok access token and refresh token are stored privately inside the operator's own automation workflow (n8n). They are used only to upload drafts and are never shared or sold.

Videos pass through the operator's own Google Drive folder on the way to TikTok. No other personal data is collected, and there are no cookies, analytics, or ads on this site.

Sharing

Nothing is shared with third parties, apart from sending the videos to TikTok, which is the whole purpose of the tool.

Removing access

Disconnect Clip Relay at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, then Security, then Apps and services permissions (menu names may vary). Once disconnected, the stored tokens stop working.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
